## Run 1 — W first · pair framing

Prompt: "These are two generative art pieces from the same algorithm family, same palette,
rendered identically. Look at them carefully as aesthetic objects. For EACH piece
separately, give: (1) aesthetic quality 1-10 (composition, focal structure, balance, how
long it rewards looking), (2) visual tension 1-10 (instability, energy, unease vs calm),
(3) two sentences of concrete visual reasoning grounded in what you actually see (density
distribution, where the eye lands, how depth reads). Then: which piece you would rather
hang side by side with its twin, and why. Commit to numbers — no hedging. Do not guess how
they were made; describe what is visible."

> **Piece 1** [W]
> (1) Aesthetic Quality: 8
> (2) Visual Tension: 4
> (3) Reasoning: The composition features a clear central anchor with radiating limbs that
> create a balanced, organic silhouette. The eye travels outward from the dense core to the
> fine, peripheral tips, providing a satisfying sense of structural growth.
>
> **Piece 2** [B]
> (1) Aesthetic Quality: 6
> (2) Visual Tension: 7
> (3) Reasoning: The density is concentrated in a chaotic central cluster, leaving the
> outer edges feeling fragmented and uneven. The lack of a strong directional flow causes
> the eye to bounce erratically around the center rather than exploring the full frame.
>
> **Comparison**
> I would rather hang Piece 1 side by side with its twin. Its superior balance and
> structural clarity provide a stable baseline that makes the frantic energy of Piece 2
> serve as a compelling foil, creating a narrative of order versus entropy.
